### Full description — máx. 4000

```
Catculator is a real calculator with a real cat living inside it.

Do ordinary maths — add, subtract, multiply, divide, percentages — while a cat watches from the top of the screen. Its eyes follow your cursor. It blinks. It flicks its tail. It gets cross if you divide by zero, and celebrates when you get a result. Leave it alone for 45 seconds and it falls asleep. Tap it and it purrs.

SCIENTIFIC MODE
Tap the microscope and the whole calculator opens up:
• Trigonometry: sin, cos, tan and their inverses, with a DEG/RAD switch
• Logarithms ln and log, plus e^x and 10^x
• Powers and roots: x², x³, x^y, √, ∛, 1/x
• Constants π and e, factorial n!, absolute value, modulo, EXP notation
• Ans to reuse your last result
• Full memory: MC, MR, M+, M−, MS, with an on-screen indicator
• Brackets, correct order of operations, and a live preview while you type
• Exact fractions: 2÷8 becomes 1/4 with one tap

UNIT CONVERTER
Length, weight, temperature, volume and speed. Metric and imperial. Tap the result to drop it straight into the calculator.

SHOPPING MODE
• Discounts: what you actually pay, and how much you saved
• VAT both ways — add it on, or strip it out of a price that already includes it
• Tips
• Split a bill between people
• Currency converter with 11 currencies. The rates are yours to edit and they work offline, and the app tells you how long ago you last updated each one

NOTEPAD
Jot down sums, lists or ideas. Drop the number on screen into your notes with one tap. It saves itself.

STUDY MODE
The cat asks, you answer. Addition, subtraction, times tables and division, getting harder as your streak grows. It remembers your record.

HISTORY
Your last 40 calculations. Tap any of them to bring the number back.

MAKE IT YOURS
• 6 colour themes: Cyan, Pink, Mint, Lavender, Tabby and Night
• 5 coats for the cat: Charcoal, Ginger, Grey, Black and White
• 6 outfits: ninja, footballer, pirate, wizard, hooded, or all natural
• English and Spanish, switchable at any time
• Synthesised cat sounds — clicks, meows, purrs and hisses — that you can turn off
• Follows your system dark mode and your reduced-motion setting
• Remembers exactly how you left it

USABLE WITHOUT LOOKING
Every button has a proper spoken name for screen readers, the result can be copied straight from the keyboard, and the animations respect your accessibility settings.

PAY ONCE AND THAT IS IT
Catculator costs about what a vending-machine coffee costs, once. In exchange:
• No ads. Ever. Not now, not in the next update
• No in-app purchases and no locked features — you get all of it
• No subscriptions
• No account and no sign-up
• No permissions: no camera, no contacts, no location, no storage
• No internet — it works completely in aeroplane mode
• Not a single piece of your data collected

Free calculators are paid for with your data, or with full-screen ads between one sum and the next. This one is paid for once, and that is the end of it.
```

## Notas al pegar

- El **título en inglés lleva "Cat Calculator"** a propósito: "Catculator" solo
  no lo busca nadie, y "calculator" es la palabra por la que sí se busca.
- La **descripción corta es lo que más pesa** en las búsquedas de Play después
  del título. Los ganchos elegidos son *no ads*, *no permissions* y *offline*,
  que es justo lo que la competencia gratuita no puede decir.
- Al agregar un idioma en Play Console hay que subirle **capturas propias**; no
  se heredan de la otra ficha.
- En Partner Center el `.appx` ya declara `es-419` y `en-US`, así que la ficha
  en inglés se espera y hoy no existe.
